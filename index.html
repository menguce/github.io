About

I am an Electrical and Electronics Engineer and Software Developer with a strong academic background in imaging and intelligent systems. I completed my M.Sc. (2018) and Ph.D. (2023) at Akdeniz University, where my research focused on image processing, computer vision, image reconstruction, microwave imaging, and biomedical imaging systems. My research integrates artificial intelligence (AI) and signal processing techniques to enhance not only image quality but also the extraction of detailed information, interpretability, and overall system performance. I have solid programming experience in Python, Django, Jupiter Notebook, MATLAB, C/C#, HTML, and CSS, which I actively apply to develop data-driven and computational solutions in my research. My academic interests extend to machine learning, computer vision, and intelligent imaging applications. I am dedicated to advancing interdisciplinary research that bridges engineering, computation, and imaging sciences to achieve innovative and impactful outcomes.
